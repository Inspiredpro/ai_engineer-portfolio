TechSupport AI sales management system

Make, Gemini, Airtable, Google Sheets, Telegram and Gmail, with a product page built and deployed on Vercel

The problemA laptop seller gets enquiries from several places, can't tell serious buyers from people just asking, forgets to follow up, and has no single record of who is where in the sale. Good leads go cold and the sales team spends its day on repetitive admin.
  • Every enquiry now flows into one Google Sheets lead database, whether it comes from the existing enquiry page or the new product page.
  • Gemini reads each enquiry and returns the customer's need, buying intent and urgency as JSON, plus a personalised reply draft.
  • Make, not the AI, calculates the lead score and sorts leads into Hot, Warm and Cold using fixed rules, so the result is consistent and easy to audit.
  • Each lead becomes an Airtable CRM record with a sales stage and a follow-up date. Hot leads alert the sales team on Telegram, and every customer gets a personalised Gmail reply.
  • A daily scenario finds leads whose follow-up is due, emails them and updates the CRM, so nobody is forgotten.

Rule-based lead scoring (out of 100)

Hot
75 to 100
Warm
45 to 74
Cold
0 to 44

Points come from buying intent, urgency, a stated budget and a clearly identified need. The AI understands the message and the rules make the decision.

How one enquiry moves through the system

  1. Customer submits the product enquiry form
  2. Server route forwards it to MakeWebhook URL kept off the browser
  3. Row added to Google Sheets
  4. Gemini analyses need, intent and urgency
  5. Make scores and labels the lead
  6. Airtable CRM record created
  7. Hot: Telegram alert. All: Gmail reply

How I work

I build AI products end to end: retrieval, model calls, human fallback, CRM integration, access control and deployment. I care about what happens when the model is wrong, so I design the handoff as carefully as the answer.

For machine learning, I compare every model against a baseline on the same validation setup and pick what ships from the metrics. I use AI coding agents (DeepSeek, Claude and ChatGPT) to move faster, then verify the results with benchmark tests.
